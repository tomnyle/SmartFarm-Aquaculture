# Operation Checklist

## Daily inspection

- Check DO reading and verify at least one aerator is available.
- Confirm water level is above the low threshold.
- Confirm feeder is not locked unexpectedly.
- Look for active alarm text in Home Assistant or MQTT.
- Listen/observe that pump and aerators physically run when commanded.

## Weekly cleaning / calibration check

- Rinse pH and DO probes according to vendor guidance.
- Inspect sensor cables, waterproof glands, and terminal corrosion.
- Test manual ON/OFF control for aerator, pump, feeder, and alarm.
- Check that relay/contactors are not overheating.

## Monthly calibration / electrical safety test

- Recalibrate the pH probe.
- Re-check DO calibration or reference reading.
- Test RCCB/GFCI function.
- Inspect grounding, surge protection, and enclosure sealing.
- Verify pump timeout and feeder timeout behavior during supervised tests.

## Emergency procedure

### DO critical / emergency
1. Confirm both aerators are running.
2. Stop or postpone feeding.
3. Inspect for clogged diffusers, power issues, or overloaded biomass.
4. If needed, add backup aeration or partial water exchange.

### Power loss
1. Move to backup power if available.
2. Prioritize aeration before non-essential loads.
3. Check water level and fish behavior once power returns.

### Water level critical
1. Verify the refill source and pump intake.
2. Keep feeder locked until water level recovers.
3. Inspect for leaks, blocked pipework, or dry-run conditions.
