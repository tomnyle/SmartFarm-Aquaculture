# Sensors

## Phase 1
- DS18B20 on 1-Wire GPIO4
- pH on ADS1115 channel A0
- Dissolved oxygen on ADS1115 channel A1
- Water level on GPIO34 ADC

## Phase 2 skeleton
- EC/TDS
- ORP
- Turbidity

## Phase 3 skeleton
- CO2
- Gas sensor for NH3/NH4+ expansion

Enable or disable sensors in `src/config/sensors_config.h`.
